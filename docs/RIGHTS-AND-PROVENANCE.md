# Kujo Beatdown — Rights, Provenance, and Map Contribution Boundary

> **Status:** Fan mapping project. This repository does not itself establish a license to Ren's recording or composition.

## Separate the map from the music

A Beat Saber map/chart is a contributor-created interactive representation. It does not automatically confer rights to the underlying recording or composition.

Keep separate records for:

1. **Source music** — the exact Ren recording/version and applicable rightsholders.
2. **Chart/map** — who created the difficulty, lightshow, timing, or other map content.
3. **Distribution** — where the compiled map/package may be shared and under what conditions.

A contributor may be able to create mapping work without the repository thereby acquiring rights to distribute the underlying recording.

## Contribution provenance

Every substantive map contribution should be traceable to:

- contributor;
- issue/task;
- difficulty or section;
- source/timing reference;
- files changed;
- testing performed;
- date/review;
- any third-party material included.

For timing references, prefer notes and metadata that can be shared lawfully rather than copying protected media into the repository.

## Music rights status

The project should not state that Ren has licensed, approved, endorsed, or participated in the map unless written evidence supports the statement.

Before a public distribution package is prepared, verify the applicable platform/community rules and the rights status of the exact recording.

The following are **not** substitutes for permission:

- fan-project status;
- noncommercial intent;
- artist attribution;
- a streaming-service page;
- a gameplay video existing elsewhere;
- a contributor's assumption that the song is "already on the internet."

## Contributor workflow

Use this order:

1. Identify the exact source recording.
2. Record its provenance and rights status.
3. Create the map/chart in a scoped issue.
4. Test the map.
5. Record contributor credit and testing evidence.
6. Confirm the permitted distribution route.
7. Package/distribute only within that confirmed boundary.

If rights status is uncertain, preserve the mapping work as a development artifact and do not treat uncertainty as authorization.

**Principle:** The map contribution can be attributable even when music rights remain unresolved.
